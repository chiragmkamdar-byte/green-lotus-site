Specifications

Star Anise grades we ship

Star anise is the dried fruit of Illicium verum, native to northeast Vietnam and southern China. The autumn crop carries the fuller flower and the higher oil content, and is what most buyers specify for whole-pod retail and for oil extraction.

Forms

The forms we ship

Star anise is bought on pod integrity and on volatile oil. Whole pods carry the premium; broken material goes to extraction at a lower price.

Whole pods

Full flowers with eight to ten petals, cleaned and sorted. Autumn crop carries the fuller pod and the higher oil, typically 8%% against 7%% for spring crop. Organic certified lots available.

Best for: retail whole spice, stocks and broths, mulling, visible garnish.

Broken

Loose petals and fragments from sorting. The same crop as the whole pods, priced lower because the shape is gone, which makes it the sensible buy if the pod is going to be milled or extracted anyway.

Best for: oil and oleoresin extraction, brewing, infusions.

Ground

Milled to your mesh from whole or broken pods. We grind to order, because the anethole that gives star anise its character is volatile and goes off in storage once the pod is opened up.

Best for: five-spice powder, seasoning blends, bakery, ready meals.

Sourcing

Origin and processing

Our star anise comes from Lang Son and Cao Bang in the northeast, harvested green and sun-dried. Autumn crop runs from August to October, spring crop from March to April.

We supply EU grade and organic certified lots, cleaned, sorted and graded by pod integrity. Broken and ground are available for extraction buyers.
