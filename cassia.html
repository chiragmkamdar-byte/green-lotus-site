Specifications

Cassia grades we ship

Cassia is the bark of the Cinnamomum cassia tree, stripped, trimmed and dried into the cuts the trade works with. Vietnamese cassia carries a higher volatile oil content than most origins, which is why grinders and oleoresin buyers ask for it by name.

Sourcing

Origin and processing

Our cassia comes from the traditional growing districts of Yen Bai, Quang Nam and Thanh Hoa, where the bark is harvested, scraped and sun-dried before grading by cut and length.

We supply split, tube, broken, chips and ground cassia, cut to the length your line runs. Coumarin and volatile oil results are available per lot for buyers shipping into the EU.
