Sourcing

Origin and processing

Raw nuts are sourced from Binh Phuoc and Dong Nai, the heart of Vietnamese cashew growing, then shelled, peeled, graded and packed at partner facilities.

Kernels are vacuum packed and cartoned to your required tin or pouch size. Metal detection and moisture checks run on every lot before sealing.
