About

Located in Vietnam, we are passionate about sourcing the highest quality spices and products from across the globe. We specialise in pepper, cassia, star anise, cashew, dried red chilli, and desiccated coconut. We strive to deliver outstanding products to our customers. We can also cater to your specific needs by sourcing additional products based on your requirements. Come and discover the vibrant flavors of the world with Green Lotus Spices!

Mission

Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality spices sourced from the finest regions in Vietnam, Indonesia, India, and China, ensuring that our customers get authentic flavors from around the globe.
