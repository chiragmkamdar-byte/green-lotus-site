Forms

The forms we ship

Chilli is bought on colour value and heat before anything else, and both are supplied with the lot analysis. The form then depends on where it goes in your process.

Whole pods, with stem or stemless

Sun-dried or machine-dried to hold colour. Stemless costs a little more to produce but saves you the sorting, and it is what most food manufacturers specify.

Best for: retail whole chilli, pastes and sauces, infusing oils.

Crushed flakes, 3 to 5 mm

Pods broken with the seed left in, so the heat stays high and the flakes stay visible.

Best for: pizza and pasta shakers, kimchi, seasoning blends.

Powder

Ground to your mesh, with or without seed depending on the heat you want. Steam sterilisation available for ready-to-eat applications.

Best for: sauces, ready meals, curry and spice blends, retail packing.

Sourcing

Origin and processing

Chilli is sourced from the Mekong Delta and the central coast, harvested ripe and either sun-dried or machine-dried to hold colour.

Cleaning, destemming, crushing and grinding run in-line, with metal detection before packing. Steam sterilisation is available for ready-to-eat applications.
