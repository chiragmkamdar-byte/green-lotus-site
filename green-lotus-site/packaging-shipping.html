Packing

How we pack

Standard export packing is food-grade woven or multi-wall bags with an inner polyethylene liner and lot marking on the outer. Cashew kernels are vacuum packed and cartoned.

Standard

  • Food-grade bags with inner liner
  • Lot marking on every outer
  • Cashews vacuum packed and cartoned
  • Palletisation on request

Custom

  • Bag weights to your specification
  • Private label — send artwork with your enquiry
  • Retail packing on request

Terms

  • Payment 20% advance, balance 80% cash against documents by T/T
  • Terms negotiable after the first shipment
  • FOB, CIF, CFR and EXW quoted on request
  • Container quantity confirmed with your quote
